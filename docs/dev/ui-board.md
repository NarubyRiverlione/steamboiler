# UI board

The presentation of the analog control-room board. Why: [ADR 0015](../adr/0015-ui-redo-analog-board-preferred.md). Only `src/components/` and `src/navigation/` change for the board; `src/context/` stays as it is.

## Tokens

All in `src/components/board/board.css`, under a `--board-*` prefix so they can't clash with other styles. `board.css` also holds the page base (border-box sizing, `body` margin and floor colour). Groups: fonts (`--board-font-stencil`, `-lcd`, `-label`), page and text, plate and panel, screws, LCD (`--board-lcd-*`, with `-alarm` for a value in alarm), LED (`--board-led-off`; lit uses `--board-lcd-alarm`), lens button and switch legends (`--board-lens-*`), switch rims (`--board-knob-valve-rim`, `-pump-rim`, `-gas-rim`), mimic (`--board-mimic-*`, one per medium). Class names carry the same `board-` prefix. Fonts: [fonts.md](fonts.md).

## Primitives

In `src/components/board/`. Each file opens with a comment saying it is adapted from MC-v2 (the `missile-boat-fire-control` repo, `src/ui`). Nothing is copied speculatively: a step copies the primitives it uses.

| Primitive | What |
|---|---|
| `PanelFrame` | Plate, inset panel, stencil title (`h1` or `h2`), four corner screws at fixed angles |
| `Display` | LCD readout (an `<output>` named by its label); red while `alarm` is set; flashes (`board-display-flash`) while `flashing` is set, as the simulated clock does while paused; `widest` is the longest text it can show, and the box keeps that width (right-aligned) so it does not resize as the value changes. Every LCD on the pages has one, sized from the peaks of the headless runs plus headroom |
| `Screws` | The four corner screws at fixed angles; used by `PanelFrame` and `Header` |
| `Led` | Round bezel LED for use inside an SVG (an `img` named "<label>: on/off"); red while `lit` |
| `Lamp` | `Led` in its own SVG with a text label, for use in HTML |
| `LensButton` | Illuminated pushbutton for a momentary action (ACK, RESET, Presets) or one of several (speed mode, run-up point, playback speed); lit while `pressed` (`aria-pressed`); `disabled` dims it, `title` carries the reason. Its label never changes; `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` can be passed (the annunciator fold toggle, the one stateful `LensButton`: it discloses a panel rather than switching a plant control, so it is not a `TurnSwitch`). A control that holds a state is a `TurnSwitch` ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-latching-controls-are-turn-switches.md)) |
| `TurnSwitch` | Two-position turn switch for a control that holds a state (`role="switch"`, `aria-checked`): a dark cap with a rim (`tone`: brass `valve`, blue `machine`, red `breaker`) and a lever whose grip sticks out of the far side of the hub, ticks at the ends of the 90° swing, and two lens-plate legends (`offLabel`, `onLabel`; the selected one is lit). Click, Space and Enter turn it; Left and Right still change the page. `disabled` refuses and `title` (an SVG `<title>`) carries the reason. `guard` adds a flip cover over the switch: down it blocks the switch and a click, Space or Enter lifts it; up, the switch turns and the strip or Escape puts the cover down (the accessible name says "cover down" or "cover up"). The drawn parts are in `TurnSwitchParts.tsx`, tick geometry in `switchGeometry.ts`, the rules for a click or key (turn, lift the cover, refuse) in `switchInput.ts` and the handlers in `useSwitchInput.ts` |
| `RoundGauge` | Round dial (`role="meter"`) with a needle and zones (`neutral` white, `ok`, `warn`, `alarm`); the unit on the face is bold (`.board-gauge-unit`, shared with the horizontal `LinearGauge`) |
| `LinearGauge` | Linear gauge on a `min`/`max` scale (default 0 to 100; `ticks`, `major`/`minor`, `zones`, `bands`, `target` and the `aria-value*` all in scale units; `role="meter"`). `orientation` `vertical` (default) is a vessel level: housing, glass, water fill, zone strip and a pointer. `horizontal` is a straight-scale meter read by a red triangle pointer above the scale, its tip on the top edge of the frame: a dial face like `RoundGauge`, the zones as a band as tall as the major ticks inside a frame (`.board-meter-frame`), the ticks over it and `bands` inside the frame, an optional `unit`, no glass or fill. Ticks: `major` and `minor` give evenly spaced ticks, labelled on the majors, as on `RoundGauge`; without them every value in `ticks` is a labelled tick. Both draw `bands` (a marked band on the glass, at least 3 px wide so a narrow one stays visible; #104) and an optional dashed `target` line across the glass: the level the vessel should hold (drum 50 %, hotwell 70 %, deaerator 50 %, `WorkingLevel` and `HotwellNormalLevel` in `const.ts`). A short-term stand-in until the green and orange bands |
| `ValveControl` | A valve knob (`Knob`, absolute mode, `role="slider"`) with the flow and `% Open` LCDs. Drag right opens, left closes (a pump knob, `Pump`, takes 480 px of drag for a full sweep against 120 px for a valve, so small adjustments are easy); wheel (one step per 50 ms, so a trackpad swipe turns it slowly) and Up and Down keys move by `Step` percent (1 for a pump, 5 for a valve). The knob sets an absolute command; `useValveRamp` turns it into `cbAdjust(delta)` calls (the actions add a delta) at 100 % per 15 real seconds (`valveRamp.ts`, a UX constant, not a plant parameter). The cap shows the command, a second pointer and the LCD show the actual position. The ramp follows the actual position: when the governor moves the turbine valve, or the reducer refuses a move, the command is dropped and the knob follows. Used by the Condenser and Turbine pages |

## Shell and navigation

In `src/navigation/`. `Layout` is the shell of every page: `Header` (a plate like the part pages: the `h1` "Powerplant Simulation", a lens button "Plant overview" lit on the overview, the breadcrumb (the name of the open page, centred; the button is the way back to the overview), the **Presets** button at the right of the nav row, the annunciator in `data-slot="annunciator"`, the headline plant values in `data-slot="values"` (`PlantValues`: two plain `Display` readouts, Boiler pressure in bar, smoothed as the Boiler page, and Output in MW from `electricOutput`; no alarm colour; two rows as tall as the ACK/RESET column; #101), and in `data-slot="clock" | "speed"` the simulated clock and playback speed selector of `TimeControl.tsx` (the clock is a `Display` that flashes, `board-display-flash`, while paused; the speeds Pause, 1x, 10x, 60x are `LensButton`s, and speeds above 1x are disabled while an annunciator tile flashes; #86)) above the routed page. There is no tab strip. The header is fixed: the shell is one screen tall (`.board-shell`, `height: 100vh`) and only `main.board-main` below it scrolls. The shell is the board floor (`--board-page-bg`, #2b2e31) with the plates on it. Every page is a board page; no old UI code or CSS is left (#98).

- **Routes** (`AppRoutes`): `/` is `PlantOverview`; `/boiler`, `/deaerator`, `/condenser`, `/turbine` the detail pages.
- **Keys** (`useBoardKeys`): Page Down or Right and Page Up or Left cycle Boiler, Deaerator, Condenser, Turbine (from the overview: first or last, ends wrap); Esc returns to the overview. A focused button or link keeps the arrows (not Esc); an unknown path redirects to the overview. Ignored with Alt, Ctrl or Meta, and while the focus is in an `input`, `textarea`, `select`, `contenteditable` or `spinbutton`. A focused knob does not keep Left and Right: they change the page, and a knob turns with Up, Down, Home, End, the wheel and drag.
- **Parts** (`plantParts.ts`): the part list (path, name) and `partAlarms`, the alarm flags per part, derived from `alarms.ts` (below); a new alarm flag is added there and gets its annunciator tile.
- **Presets** (`PresetsButton`, in the header nav row): a lens button that opens a modal popup (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`) listing the start states of `START_PRESETS` (`src/context/startPresets.ts`): Cold & dark (`makeInitialState()`), Hot standby (the `bypass-loop` snapshot) and At pressure (the `turbine-runup` snapshot). Choosing one dispatches `LOAD_STATE` (the reducer returns the payload as the whole new state), clears the annunciator latches (`clearLatches`) and closes the popup; there is no confirmation. Close button, Escape and a click on the backdrop close it without touching the plant. Focus moves into the popup, Tab wraps inside it and focus returns to the button on close. While it is open, Esc and Page Up/Down are handled by the popup and do not navigate (`useBoardKeys` honours `defaultPrevented`). The snapshots are imported as JSON through Vite from `sim/snapshots/` (`resolveJsonModule`) and checked with `shapeErrors` (`src/utils/stateShape.ts`, shared with the runner) so a stale snapshot throws instead of loading. The app still starts cold. The clock, speed and Graphs-history resets of #78 and #36 join `LOAD_STATE` handling when those tickets land.
- **Annunciator** (`Annunciator`, in the header; #117): folded by default behind a toggle lens button (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) with two **master lights** beside it (`masterLights.ts`: red is lit when any error tile is lit, yellow when any warning tile is lit and no error is; a master flashes while any tile of its severity flashes and is steady once all are acknowledged; `role="img"`, `data-state` off, flashing or steady). ACK and RESET sit in the header, always visible, and act on all tiles. The unfolded tile panel is an absolute overlay under the header (the shell and pages keep their size), the tiles grouped by owning part (Boiler, Condenser, Turbine; a part without an alarm shows no group). Esc, a click outside or opening a part folds it; Esc is handled before the board keys, so it does not also go to the overview. A new annunciation never opens the panel. The speed lock reads the provider's tiles, so it works folded. The tiles, one per alarm flag, listed in `alarms.ts` (id, label, severity, owning part, flag): Heating rate over limit and Burner trip (Boiler), Condensation pump trip (Condenser), Turbine trip (Turbine), Hotwell level low and Hotwell level high (Condenser, #59: derived from the level, below 25% and from 85%, no state), Boiler level high (warning) and Boiler level high-high trip (error) (Boiler, #62: derived from the level from 65%, and the flag of the trip at 70%) (the overspeed trip, #72, sets `turbineTripped` and adds no tile). Tiles **latch** (#116). A new trip flashes (`board-tile-flash`, slower under `prefers-reduced-motion`) until ACK; ACK makes a flashing tile steady. A flag that clears while the tile still flashes (unacknowledged) darkens it; one that clears while the tile is steady leaves it lit. RESET, which replaces the old TEST (the lamp test is gone), darkens a steady tile only when its flag is clear; while the flag is set RESET does nothing, and a flashing tile is not touched. A flag that re-trips while the tile is steady keeps it steady. The speed lock above 1x reads only flashing tiles, so a steady latched tile does not lock the speed; loading a preset clears all latches. A tile is a button that opens the part that owns the alarm. The state (per tile `flashing` or `steady`, none is dark) lives in `AnnunciatorProvider` / `useAnnunciator` in `src/navigation/`, a React context wrapped by `Layout` that reads the plant state; nothing under `src/context/` changes. **Severity** (#115): every alarm declares `severity: "error" | "warning"` (required by the `Alarm` type, so a new alarm cannot omit it). A tile gets `board-tile-error` (red backlight) or `board-tile-warning` (yellow), lit steady and while flashing alike; the dark phase of the flash and an unlit tile use the off colours. Heating rate over limit is a warning; Burner, Condensation pump and Turbine trip are errors; Hotwell level low and high and Boiler level high are warnings; Boiler level high-high trip is an error.  Classes added by #117: `board-annun-bar`, `board-master`, `board-annun-panel` (absolute, `z-index` 50 over the header's 20) and `board-annun-group-title`. A master follows the lit tiles, so a steady latched tile whose flag has cleared keeps it lit until RESET. Tokens: `--board-tile-off-*`, `--board-tile-error-{bg,text,glow}`, `--board-tile-warning-{bg,text,glow}`, `--board-annun-well`.
- **Overview** (`PlantOverview`, data in `plantDiagram.ts`): the plant of [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-target-plant.svg), laid out as in the full-board mockup, as an SVG with one set of live values: the Deaerator node shows the water temperature and the level % (`PlantOverview`, from `state.Deaerator`) and, while the start-up vent is open, the safe-to-close lamp (an `ok` `Led` on the node's top right). A node with a `symbol` (`valve`: Main steam valve, Turbine valve, Bypass valve, Let-down valve; `pump`: Condensation pump, Boiler feed pump, Recirculation pump, Air extraction pump) is drawn as its industry symbol (bowtie, circle with a triangle) on a line through the node's box, the name above it (or, with `label: "left"`, left of it; `labelDx` shifts it right, as on the air extraction pump, whose name is shortened to "extraction" to clear the bypass value), and `flow` (right, `left`, `down`) giving the direction; the box only joins the pipes and takes the click, so such a node is not raised. A node with a `part` is a link to that page and drawn raised (Boiler: Burner, Boiler, Drain; Turbine: Main steam valve, Turbine valve, Turbine, Generator, Bypass valve; Condenser: Condenser, pumps, SJAE, Hotwell; Deaerator: Deaerator, Boiler feed pump, Let-down valve); the node marked `led` carries the part's alarm LED. The deaerator sits in the water path hotwell → condensation pump → deaerator → boiler feed pump → extraction heater → economizer: hotwell, condensation pump, deaerator, feed pump and heater stand on one row, right to left, and the heater drain (labelled) returns under the pump to the deaerator bottom; the auxiliary steam line from the main steam pipe carries the let-down valve and, joined by the extraction branch (turbine tap; the non-return valve is in the documentation schema only), enters the deaerator top (#118; the temporary pass-through of #40 is never drawn). The other nodes are flat; the extraction heater has no controls, the air preheater is dashed. **Live values** (#99): `DIAGRAM_VALUES` in `plantDiagram.ts` lists each value (node, label, anchor in diagram units, `widest`); `overviewValues.ts` turns the state into the texts; `PlantOverview` draws each as a small `Display` in a `foreignObject` at diagram scale (`.board-overview-value`, 14 px), pointer events off so the node links still work. Values: Boiler pressure and level, Main steam valve OPEN or CLOSED, Turbine valve and Bypass valve position, Turbine speed, Load setpoint (MW, above the Load node), Condenser pressure, Hotwell level, Condensation pump position, and the output temperature of the Extraction heater (`Heater.returnTemperature`), Economizer (`Boiler.returnTemperatureOut`) and Stack. Pressure and the hotwell level are smoothed with `useEMA` at the same alpha as the Boiler and Condenser pages (0.01, 0.1); the drum level is `waterVolume / TotalVolume`, unsmoothed as on the Boiler page, so the numbers match. A **Values** `LensButton` (top right of the plate, lit while shown) hides or shows all labels, the Deaerator's temperature and level included; it is UI state that starts on at every load, and the safe-to-close lamp stays. No pipe colour or animation, now or planned. When the plant changes, change `plantDiagram.ts` too.

## Building a part page

A part page is a `PanelFrame` holding its readouts and controls, each a board primitive fed from the plant state; an action is dispatched through the existing reducer. Copy a primitive from MC-v2 into `src/components/board/` when a page needs it, with its tokens in `board.css`, and a test in the `ui` project ([testing.md](testing.md)) that checks behaviour. Each page is checked by eye against the full-board mockup in the ADR; there is no visual-regression tooling.

The status panel is a grid of rows (`.board-readouts` > `.board-readout`): a label in a fixed-width column (10.5 rem) and the value right after it, so a value never sits closer to the next column's label than to its own. Give every LCD a `widest`, so nothing resizes while the plant runs. A control that holds a state is a `TurnSwitch`, never a `LensButton` whose label changes.

The Boiler status panel uses three fixed columns (`.board-readouts-cols` > `.board-readout-col`): temperature and energy change; stack temperature, boiler efficiency and burner trip; heating rate and relief valve. The last two put the label above, with the lamp and the LCD side by side below it (`.board-readout-stacked`).
