# UI board

The presentation of the analog control-room board. Why: [ADR 0015](../adr/0015-ui-redo-analog-board-preferred.md). Only `src/components/` and `src/navigation/` change for the board; `src/context/` stays as it is.

## Tokens

All in `src/components/board/board.css`, under a `--board-*` prefix so they can't clash with other styles. `board.css` also holds the page base (border-box sizing, `body` margin and floor colour). Groups: fonts (`--board-font-stencil`, `-lcd`, `-label`), page and text, plate and panel, screws, LCD (`--board-lcd-*`, with `-alarm` for a value in alarm), LED (`--board-led-off`; lit uses `--board-lcd-alarm`), lens button and switch legends (`--board-lens-*`), switch rims (`--board-knob-valve-rim`, `-pump-rim`, `-gas-rim`), mimic (`--board-mimic-*`, one per medium). Class names carry the same `board-` prefix. Fonts: [fonts.md](fonts.md).

## Primitives

In `src/components/board/`. Each file opens with a comment saying it is adapted from MC-v2 (the `missile-boat-fire-control` repo, `src/ui`). Nothing is copied speculatively: a step copies the primitives it uses.

| Primitive | What |
|---|---|
| `PanelFrame` | Plate, inset panel, stencil title (`h1` or `h2`), four corner screws at fixed angles |
| `Display` | LCD readout (an `<output>` named by its label); red while `alarm` is set; flashes (`board-display-flash`) while `flashing` is set, as the simulated clock does while paused; blank (empty text) whenever the board state is not normal (vital bus dead, #144) unless `exempt` is set, as on the simulated clock; `widest` is the longest text it can show, and the box keeps that width (right-aligned) so it does not resize as the value changes. Every LCD on the pages has one, sized from the peaks of the headless runs plus headroom |
| `Screws` | The four corner screws at fixed angles; used by `PanelFrame` and `Header` |
| `Led` | Round bezel LED for use inside an SVG (an `img` named "<label>: on/off"); red while `lit` |
| `Lamp` | `Led` in its own SVG with a text label, for use in HTML |
| `LensButton` | Illuminated pushbutton for a momentary action (ACK, annunciator RESET, trip RESET, Presets) or one of several (speed mode, run-up point, playback speed); lit while `pressed` (`aria-pressed`); `disabled` dims it, `title` carries the reason. Its label never changes; `aria-label` (the − and + fine-tune buttons of `ValveControl`, #138), `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` can be passed (the annunciator fold toggle, the one stateful `LensButton`: it discloses a panel rather than switching a plant control, so it is not a `TurnSwitch`). A control that holds a state is a `TurnSwitch` ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-latching-controls-are-turn-switches.md)) |
| `TripResetButton` | The RESET `LensButton` beside a trip lamp (`trip` id, `label`): reads the plant from the context, enabled only while its trip is latched and `tripResetRefusal` is `undefined`, otherwise disabled with the reason as `title`; dispatches `RESET_TRIP` ([ADR 0043](../adr/0043-trip-reset.md)) |
| `TurnSwitch` | Two-position turn switch for a control that holds a state (`role="switch"`, `aria-checked`): a dark cap with a rim (`tone`: brass `valve`, blue `machine`) and a lever whose grip sticks out of the far side of the hub, ticks at the ends of the 90° swing, and two lens-plate legends (`offLabel`, `onLabel`; the selected one is lit). Click, Space and Enter turn it; Left and Right still change the page. `disabled` refuses and `title` (an SVG `<title>`) carries the reason. `guard` adds a flip cover over the switch. The drawn parts are in `TurnSwitchParts.tsx`, tick geometry in `switchGeometry.ts`, the rules for a click or key (turn, lift the cover, refuse) in `switchInput.ts` and the handlers in `useSwitchInput.ts` |
| `BreakerSwitch` | Heavy breaker (#172, ADR 0039) with the props of `TurnSwitch` (`on`, `onToggle`, `disabled`, `title`, `guard`) plus `plate` and `rating` (dummy nameplate text, not modelled): a cast body with two screws, a nameplate, a big position flag (green **O OPEN**, red **I CLOSED** from `--board-breaker-open` and `--board-breaker-closed`; dimmed while refused) and a rocker in a recessed well. One focusable `role="switch"` (the well; `aria-checked`, `title` carries the reason, the accessible name says "cover down" or "cover up"). Cover down it is smoked and blocks the handle: a click, Space or Enter lifts it, even when the breaker refuses to close; up, it is swung on its hinge at the top of the well over the nameplate and flag (translucent, as wide as the nameplate) and the handle rocks; a click on the lifted cover or Escape (on this breaker only) lowers it. No instruction text on the cover. About 250 px wide, shrinks to the width of its container. Parts in `BreakerSwitchParts.tsx`, input shared via `useSwitchInput.ts` |
| `RoundGauge` | Round dial (`role="meter"`) with a needle and zones (`neutral` white, `ok`, `warn`, `alarm`); the unit on the face is bold (`.board-gauge-unit`, shared with the horizontal `LinearGauge`); an optional `setpoint` (scale units, clamped to the scale) draws a second, outlined needle (`.board-setpoint`, `--board-setpoint`) beside the solid one, and then `aria-valuetext` reads "value unit, setpoint n unit" while `aria-valuenow` stays the actual value (#105) |
| `LinearGauge` | Linear gauge on a `min`/`max` scale (default 0 to 100; `ticks`, `major`/`minor`, `zones`, `bands`, `target` and the `aria-value*` all in scale units; `role="meter"`). `orientation` `vertical` (default) is a vessel level: housing, glass, water fill, zone strip and a pointer. `horizontal` is a straight-scale meter read by a red triangle pointer above the scale, its tip on the top edge of the frame: a dial face like `RoundGauge`, the zones as a band as tall as the major ticks inside a frame (`.board-meter-frame`), the ticks over it and `bands` inside the frame, an optional `unit`, no glass or fill. Ticks: `major` and `minor` give evenly spaced ticks, labelled on the majors, as on `RoundGauge`; without them every value in `ticks` is a labelled tick. Both draw `bands` (a marked band on the glass, at least 3 px wide so a narrow one stays visible; #104) and an optional dashed `target` line across the glass: the level the vessel should hold (drum 50 %, hotwell 70 %, deaerator 50 %, `WorkingLevel` and `HotwellNormalLevel` in `const.ts`). A short-term stand-in until the green and orange bands |
| `ValveControl` | A valve knob (`Knob`, absolute mode, `role="slider"`) with the flow and `% Open` LCDs. Drag right opens, left closes (a pump knob, `Pump`, takes 480 px of drag for a full sweep against 120 px for a valve, so small adjustments are easy); wheel (one step per 50 ms, so a trackpad swipe turns it slowly) and Up and Down keys move by `Step` percent (5 for a valve, 1 for the recirculation pump; the condensation and feed pumps use 100/300 %, 1 l/s of their 300 l/s max, about 1 kg/s per click, #138). A **−** button on the left and a **+** on the right (`LensButton`s, "… knob decrease" / "… knob increase", #138) move the command by one `Step` too: an off-grid command is first snapped to the nearest step (an exact .5 tie rounds up; `fineStep.ts`), so 6.4 % gives 5 or 7 for a pump and 0 or 10 for a valve; clamped at 0 and 100 %, where the button is disabled. The knob sets an absolute command under its `ValveId`; `ValveRampProvider` (in `Layout`, wired to the plant by `PlantValveRamp`, [ADR 0042](../adr/0042-valve-ramp-lives-above-the-pages.md), #127) turns it into the plant's adjust actions (they add a delta) at 100 % per 15 real seconds, real time at every playback speed (`valveRamp.ts`, a UX constant, not a plant parameter), so the ramp goes on while another page is shown. A new knob needs a row in `PlantValveRamp`. The cap shows the command, a second pointer and the LCD show the actual position. The ramp follows the actual position: when the governor moves the turbine valve, or the reducer refuses a move, the command is dropped and the knob follows. Used by the Condenser, Deaerator and Turbine pages `Disabled` (a reason, #149): the plant moves the valve, so the knob turns display-only, − and + are disabled with the reason as `title` and the reason shows under the knob (the spray valve in Auto). |

## Level zones

`levelZones({ trip, alarm, working })` in `board/levelZones.ts` builds the `GaugeZone[]` (percent) for the level instrument of every vessel from limits given as volume fractions; every part and every side is optional. Red is the trip and beyond, orange runs from the alarm up to the trip, green is the working band, and the buffer between has no colour. `zoneOf(level, zones)` returns the zone of a level (`"ok"`, `"warn"`, `"alarm"` or `"none"`): green holds both ends, the other zones their lower end, so a low trip is "below the limit" and a high alarm "from the limit", as in the sim. The limits are named constants in `src/context/const.ts`, the UI never imports from `sim/`. Boiler, Condenser and Deaerator pages each call the builder once at module level.

## Shell and navigation

In `src/navigation/`. `Layout` is the shell of every page in the normal and emergency board states (the dark and ended states have no header, see [Board states](#board-states); in the emergency state the annunciator slot is absent): `Header` (a plate like the part pages: the `h1` "Powerplant Simulation", a lens button "Plant overview" lit on the overview, the breadcrumb (the name of the open page, centred; the button is the way back to the overview), the **Presets** button at the right of the nav row, the annunciator in `data-slot="annunciator"`, the headline plant values in `data-slot="values"` (`PlantValues`: two plain `Display` readouts, Boiler pressure in bar, smoothed as the Boiler page, and Output in MW from `electricOutput`; no alarm colour; two rows as tall as the ACK/RESET column; #101), and in `data-slot="clock" | "speed"` the simulated clock and playback speed selector of `TimeControl.tsx` (the clock is a `Display` that flashes, `board-display-flash`, while paused; the speeds Pause, 1x, 10x, 60x are `LensButton`s, and speeds above 1x are disabled while an annunciator tile flashes; #86)) above the routed page. There is no tab strip. The header is fixed: the shell is one screen tall (`.board-shell`, `height: 100vh`) and only `main.board-main` below it scrolls. The shell is the board floor (`--board-page-bg`, #2b2e31) with the plates on it. Every page is a board page; no old UI code or CSS is left (#98).

- **Routes** (`AppRoutes`): `/` is `PlantOverview`; `/electrical` is the `ElectricalOverview` (not a part: out of `PARTS` and the Page Up/Down cycle); `/boiler`, `/steam`, `/deaerator`, `/condenser`, `/turbine`, `/turbine-aux`, `/generation`, `/auxiliary-bus`, `/diesel` the detail pages.
- **Keys** (`useBoardKeys`): Page Down or Right and Page Up or Left cycle Boiler, Steam, Deaerator, Condenser, Turbine, Turbine aux, Generation, Auxiliary bus, Diesel generator (from the overview: first or last, ends wrap); Esc, `h` (lowercase) or Backspace returns to the overview. A focused button or link keeps the arrows (not Esc, `h` or Backspace); an unknown path redirects to the overview. Ignored with Alt, Ctrl or Meta, and while the focus is in an `input`, `textarea`, `select`, `contenteditable` or `spinbutton`. A focused knob does not keep Left and Right: they change the page, and a knob turns with Up, Down, Home, End, the wheel and drag.
- **Parts** (`plantParts.ts`): the part list (path, name) and `partAlarms`, the alarm flags per part, derived from `alarms.ts` (below); a new alarm flag is added there and gets its annunciator tile.
- **Presets** (`PresetsButton`, in the header nav row): a lens button that opens a modal popup (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`) listing the start states of `START_PRESETS` (`src/context/startPresets.ts`): Cold & dark (`makeUiStartState()`: the cold plant with a 40% drum, what the app starts from, ADR 0018), Hot standby (the `bypass-loop` snapshot) and At pressure (the `turbine-runup` snapshot). Choosing one dispatches `LOAD_STATE` (the reducer returns the payload as the whole new state), clears the annunciator latches (`clearLatches`) and closes the popup; there is no confirmation. Close button, Escape and a click on the backdrop close it without touching the plant. Focus moves into the popup, Tab wraps inside it and focus returns to the button on close. While it is open, Esc, `h`, Backspace and Page Up/Down are handled by the popup and do not navigate (`useBoardKeys` honours `defaultPrevented`). The snapshots are imported as JSON through Vite from `sim/snapshots/` (`resolveJsonModule`) and checked with `shapeErrors` (`src/utils/stateShape.ts`, shared with the runner) so a stale snapshot throws instead of loading. The app still starts cold. The clock and speed resets of #78 join `LOAD_STATE` handling when that ticket lands.
- **Annunciator** (`Annunciator`, in the header; #117): folded by default behind a toggle lens button (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) with two **master lights** beside it (`masterLights.ts`: red is lit when any error tile is lit, yellow when any warning tile is lit and no error is; a master flashes while any tile of its severity flashes and is steady once all are acknowledged; `role="img"`, `data-state` off, flashing or steady). ACK and RESET sit in the header, always visible, and act on all tiles. The unfolded tile panel is a full-width overlay under the header (#156: edge to edge, a board plate with four screws (`Screws`) and an inset `--board-panel-bg` panel like the part panels (`board-annun-inset`, the part that scrolls; 36 px corners and 1 rem plate padding keep the outline clear of the screws), no side or top border, the plate edge line and shadow below; the shell and pages keep their size; capped at the viewport height minus `--board-annun-header-space` and scrolling inside; tile grid `auto-fill`, 14 rem minimum; lit tiles get a dark ring so the glow reads on the grey), the tiles grouped by owning part (Boiler, Deaerator, Condenser, Turbine, Turbine aux; a part without an alarm, such as Generation, Auxiliary bus or Diesel generator, shows no group). Esc, a click outside or opening a part folds it; Esc is handled before the board keys, so it does not also go to the overview. A new annunciation never opens the panel. The speed lock reads the provider's tiles, so it works folded. The tiles, one per alarm flag, listed in `alarms.ts` (id, label, severity, owning part, flag): Heating rate over limit and Burner trip (Boiler), Condensation pump trip (Condenser), Turbine trip (Turbine), Hotwell level low and Hotwell level high (Condenser, #59: derived from the level, below 25% and from 85%, no state) and Hotwell level high-high (error, Condenser, #157: derived, from 95% where the overfill clamp acts), Boiler level high (warning) and Boiler level high-high trip (error) (Boiler, #62: derived from the level from 65%, and the flag of the trip at 70%) Boiler level low (warning, Boiler, #137: derived, below 35%), Deaerator level low (warning, below 25%), Deaerator level high (warning, from 75%), Deaerator level high-high trip (error, follows `deaeratorHighHighTripped`, #129) and Feed pump trip (error, follows `feedPumpTripped`), all Deaerator (#137) (the overspeed trip, #72, sets `turbineTripped` and adds no tile) Gland steam shut off (warning, Turbine aux, #177: follows `turbine.glandSteamShutOff`, set by the plant when the rotor stops with the steam on, cleared when the steam is admitted again) and Lube oil pressure low (warning, Turbine aux, #160: derived, lit while the header pressure is below the permissive limit; no state flag, so the cold plant with the pump off starts lit, as on a real plant; the low-oil trip itself shows as the Turbine trip tile). Tiles **latch** (#116). A new trip flashes (`board-tile-flash`, slower under `prefers-reduced-motion`) until ACK; ACK makes a flashing tile steady. A flag that clears while the tile still flashes (unacknowledged) darkens it; one that clears while the tile is steady leaves it lit. RESET, which replaces the old TEST (the lamp test is gone), darkens a steady tile only when its flag is clear; while the flag is set RESET does nothing, and a flashing tile is not touched. A flag that re-trips while the tile is steady keeps it steady. The speed lock above 1x reads only flashing tiles, so a steady latched tile does not lock the speed; loading a preset clears all latches. A tile is a button that opens the part that owns the alarm. The state (per tile `flashing` or `steady`, none is dark) lives in `AnnunciatorProvider` / `useAnnunciator` in `src/navigation/`, a React context wrapped by `Layout` that reads the plant state; nothing under `src/context/` changes. **Severity** (#115): every alarm declares `severity: "error" | "warning"` (required by the `Alarm` type, so a new alarm cannot omit it). A tile gets `board-tile-error` (red backlight) or `board-tile-warning` (yellow), lit steady and while flashing alike; the dark phase of the flash and an unlit tile use the off colours. Heating rate over limit is a warning; Burner, Condensation pump and Turbine trip are errors; Hotwell level low and high, Boiler level high and low, Deaerator level low and high and Gland steam shut off are warnings; Boiler level high-high trip, Deaerator level high-high trip, Hotwell level high-high (#157) and Deaerator Feed pump trip are errors.  Classes added by #117: `board-annun-bar`, `board-master`, `board-annun-panel` (absolute from the header, `z-index` 50 over the header's 20) and `board-annun-group-title`. A master follows the lit tiles, so a steady latched tile whose flag has cleared keeps it lit until RESET. Tokens: `--board-tile-off-*` (the off background is `--board-lens-bg`, the unlit colour of the lens buttons), `--board-tile-error-{bg,text,glow}`, `--board-tile-warning-{bg,text,glow}`, `--board-annun-header-space`.
- **Overview** (`PlantOverview`, data in `plantDiagram.ts`): the plant of [ADR 0029](../adr/0029-target-plant.svg), laid out as in the full-board mockup, as an SVG with one set of live values: the Deaerator node shows the water temperature and the level % as live values (below) and, while the start-up vent is open, the safe-to-close lamp (an `ok` `Led` on the node's top right). A node with a `symbol` (`valve`: Main steam valve, Turbine valve, Bypass valve, Let-down valve; `pump`: Condensation pump, Boiler feed pump, Recirculation pump, Air extraction pump) is drawn as its industry symbol (bowtie, circle with a triangle) on a line through the node's box, the name above it (or, with `label: "left"`, left of it; `labelDx` shifts it right, as on the air extraction pump, whose name is shortened to "extraction" to clear the bypass value), and `flow` (right, `left`, `down`) giving the direction; the box only joins the pipes and takes the click, so such a node is not raised. A node with a `part` is a link to that page and drawn raised (Boiler: Burner, Boiler, Drain; Steam: Superheater; Turbine: Main steam valve, Turbine valve, Turbine, Bypass valve; Turbine aux: Turbine aux (above the Turbine, the `oil` medium in green `--board-mimic-oil`, carries the part's LED, #160); Generation: Generator (its Output value stays beside it, #134); Condenser: Condenser, pumps, SJAE, Hotwell; Deaerator: Deaerator, Boiler feed pump, Let-down valve); the node marked `led` carries the part's alarm LED, except the Superheater, whose `alarm: "steamTemperatureHigh"` lights its LED from that one alarm alone (labelled "Steam temperature high alarm"; #153). The deaerator sits in the water path hotwell → condensation pump → deaerator → boiler feed pump → extraction heater → economizer: hotwell, condensation pump, deaerator, feed pump and heater stand on one row, right to left, and the heater drain (labelled) returns under the pump to the deaerator bottom; the auxiliary steam line from the main steam pipe carries the let-down valve and, joined by the extraction branch (turbine tap; the non-return valve is in the documentation schema only), enters the deaerator top (#118; the temporary pass-through of #40 is never drawn). The other nodes are flat; the extraction heater has no controls, the air preheater and the reheater are dashed (`parked`, no link, no value, each with a legend line at the foot). **Gas column** (#153): burner, Superheater, Reheater (parked, #150), boiler surface, economizer, air preheater, stack; the flue gas pipe runs through them in that order. The steam line leaves the boiler's top into the Superheater and runs on to the main steam valve, labelled "superheated steam"; the Superheater node opens the Steam page. There is no relief valve and no Atmosphere on the overview (they stay in the simulation and on the Boiler page, ADR 0005). **Live values** (#99): `DIAGRAM_VALUES` in `plantDiagram.ts` lists each value (node, label, anchor in diagram units, `widest`); `overviewValues.ts` turns the state into the texts; `PlantOverview` draws each as a small `Display` in a `foreignObject` at diagram scale (`.board-overview-value`, 14 px), pointer events off so the node links still work. Values: Gas flow (L/s, right of the Burner), Steam temperature (`Boiler.steamTemperature`, beside the Superheater; a dash "—" until it is more than 1 K above the saturation temperature of the boiler pressure, because at rest the model holds saturation, 100 °C in the vented drum, with no steam to superheat), Boiler pressure and level, Main steam valve OPEN or CLOSED, Turbine valve and Bypass valve position, Turbine speed, Output (MW, above the Generator node), Load setpoint (MW, above the Load node), Condenser pressure, Hotwell level, Condensation pump and Boiler feed pump position, the Deaerator's temperature and level (inside its box), and the output temperature of the Extraction heater (`Heater.returnTemperature`), Economizer (`Boiler.returnTemperatureOut`) and Stack. Pressure is smoothed with `useEMA` at the same time constant as the Boiler page (`ReadoutSmoothingSeconds`); the hotwell level and the drum level (`waterVolume / TotalVolume`) are raw, as on their pages, so the numbers match. A **Values** `LensButton` (inside the diagram, left of the Gas supply; lit while shown) hides or shows all labels; it is UI state that starts on at every load, and the safe-to-close lamp stays. No pipe colour or animation, now or planned. When the plant changes, change `plantDiagram.ts` too.

## Boiler page

`src/components/Boiler/Boiler.tsx`, one `PanelFrame`: a three-column grid (`1fr auto 1.6fr`: the gas column is only as wide as its knob and LCD, so the drum level and the Water Valve Control share one row): the pressure `RoundGauge` (0 to 200 bar; green to `ValveRatingPressure`, orange to `ReliefOpenPressure`, red above), the gas controls (the latching rate `Knob` in `centre-zero` mode, with an LCD of the actual gas flow in L/s (the one flow that stays in volume: gas is a design value in L/s, [design-basis.md](../plant/design-basis.md), #123) and a RISING, FALLING or HOLDING indicator for a forgotten knob, #15; the valve knobs use the `absolute` mode), the drum `LinearGauge` (one red zone below `BurnerLowLowLevel`) with an LCD of the drum water change in kg/s, signed with + when filling (`deltaWaterMass / DeltaTime`, the net of feed inflow, steam and drain, #121; smoothed with `useEMA`; the litre readout is gone, #106), and the drain valve `TurnSwitch` (`CLOSED | OPEN`), then the status readouts as `Display`s with `Lamp`s for heating rate over limit, relief valve open, burner tripped and boiler high-high tripped (a `TripResetButton` beside each trip lamp, [ADR 0043](../adr/0043-trip-reset.md)), then the stack temperature and boiler efficiency (#89), and an Advanced `<details>`. Both water-change LCDs (here and on the Condenser page) format through `formatSigned` in `src/utils/`: `+` for a rise, no sign when the value rounds to zero (#111).

## Building a part page

A part page is a `PanelFrame` holding its readouts and controls, each a board primitive fed from the plant state; an action is dispatched through the existing reducer. Copy a primitive from MC-v2 into `src/components/board/` when a page needs it, with its tokens in `board.css`, and a test in the `ui` project ([testing.md](testing.md)) that checks behaviour. Each page is checked by eye against the full-board mockup in the ADR; there is no visual-regression tooling.

The status panel is a grid of rows (`.board-readouts` > `.board-readout`): a label in a fixed-width column (10.5 rem) and the value right after it, so a value never sits closer to the next column's label than to its own. Give every LCD a `widest`, so nothing resizes while the plant runs. A control that holds a state is a `TurnSwitch`, never a `LensButton` whose label changes.

The Boiler status panel uses three fixed columns (`.board-readouts-cols` > `.board-readout-col`): temperature and energy change; stack temperature, boiler efficiency and burner trip; heating rate and relief valve. The last two put the label above, with the lamp and the LCD side by side below it (`.board-readout-stacked`).

## Electrical overview

`src/navigation/ElectricalOverview.tsx`, drawn from `electricalDiagram.ts` (#171), at `/electrical`, reached by the **Electrical overview** lens button right after Plant overview in the header (`board-lens`, lit with `aria-current="page"`; the breadcrumb names the page). `ELECTRICAL_PATH` stands next to `OVERVIEW_PATH`; the page is not a part. Read-only: a single-line schematic in `PanelFrame`, `power` medium, with no control and no rule of its own; every lit, dimmed, open or closed state is read through `isBusLive`, `isVitalBusLive`, `isSafetyBusLive`, the breaker flags, `dieselOutput` and `consumerDraws`.

- **Sources (#189):** the Grid box on top with a tie rail (always lit: the grid is up for now). The grid supply breaker hangs on the rail and runs down to the Auxiliary bus (`data-source="grid"`, the station supply, no export). The generator column goes up to the grid: the generator breaker joins the rail to the Generator box (`data-source="generator-tie"`, lit while the generator breaker is closed), and a unit aux branch runs from the Generator box down through the unit aux breaker to the Auxiliary bus (`data-source="generator"`, lit while the unit aux breaker is closed). The Diesel generator box has its breaker and line to the bus (`data-source="diesel"`). Each breaker is drawn as an IEC 60617 circuit breaker (`BreakerSymbol`): the line broken by a switch blade with a cross on the upper contact, black, with the blade on the line when closed and swung off it when open, drawn at 150% of the 30 unit footprint (`BREAKER_SCALE`); a line is lit while its breaker is closed, dimmed while open. A box or breaker that links (every breaker, the Generator, the Diesel generator, the UPS, every consumer) is never dimmed and keeps the raised white look of a clickable node; the Grid box has no link and stays lit. The symbol is black in both states, so only the pipes carry the source colour. The diesel carries a MW label (`dieselOutput`).
- **Auxiliary bus:** one busbar joined by the grid supply, unit aux and diesel breakers; below it the six consumers, largest rated first, each with a `Led` lit while its draw is above 0 and its MW (`consumerDraws`, pure, in `AuxiliaryLoad.ts`; `auxiliaryLoadOf` is their sum). The Auxiliary load total sits under the list.
- **Vital bus:** fed from the Auxiliary bus, with one load below it, **Instrumentation** (LCD readouts, annunciator, controller setpoints), and **Safety bus:** fed by a line from the Auxiliary bus (the rectifier, `data-feed="safety-ac"`, dimmed with the Auxiliary bus) and by the UPS through the battery breaker, with the UPS charge in percent (`data-feed="safety"`, dimmed with the breaker open or the charge at 0; the UPS box links, so it stays lit); the bus is dimmed when both feeds are dead. Its one load is **Emergency equipment** (breaker control, trip reset coils, emergency light). - **Links:** the grid supply breaker, the unit aux breaker, the UPS and the battery breaker open the Auxiliary bus page; the generator and its breaker open Generation; the diesel and its breaker open the Diesel generator page; each consumer opens the page with its controls (Boiler feed pump: Deaerator; Recirculation, Condensation and Air extraction pump: Condenser; Lube oil pump and Turning gear motor: Turbine aux).

- **Values toggle** hides the value labels (as on the plant overview). On the emergency board the `Display`s are blank and the mimic stays readable (#144).

## Board states

The board has four states (#144, ADR 0055), derived by `deriveBoardState` (`src/navigation/boardState.ts`, a pure function of the plant state) and chosen once in `Layout`, which puts it in `BoardStateContext` (`src/components/board/boardStateContext.ts`). No part page knows about the buses; `Display` reads the context.

| Board state | When | What the board shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Normal** | vital bus live | the board as today |
| **Emergency** (red lens) | vital bus dead, safety bus live | red emergency light (`board-emergency`); every page and the overview reachable; analog gauges and lamps only: every `Display` is blank except the clock (`exempt`) and so are the header plant values; no annunciator; the `UpsGroup` in a column (`board-ups-strip`) beside the page, in `board-body` with `main`, on every page (on the Auxiliary bus page its own group serves) |
| **Dark** | vital bus dead, safety bus dead (Auxiliary bus dead, and the battery breaker open or charge 0%) | dark except the battery breaker switch (`UpsGroup breakerOnly`), the clock and the time controls |
| **Ended** | `simulationEnded` (UPS discharged to 0%) | the board frozen dark with one end plate (the app speaking, so ADR 0054's no-text rule does not apply) and the Presets (new game) button |

Order: `simulationEnded` → ended; vital bus live → normal; safety bus live → emergency; otherwise dark. **Annunciator across an outage:** `AnnunciatorProvider` treats a dead vital bus as no active alarms and drops its latches, so nothing is raised while it is dead, and at restore every active condition shows as a new, unacknowledged tile; a condition that cleared during the outage does not return. The code has no horn, so there is none to silence.
