# Review, 27 November 2026

Frozen record of the state of the sim on `dev` at `d7d1f2c`. Three questions: (A) are the ADRs built as described,
(B) is the simulation realistic with the playback speed as the only workaround, (C) are the major components in place.
Nothing in the repo was changed. Work that follows from it goes into tickets; see [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md).

Read: all 56 ADRs, `design-basis.md`, `real-plant-gaps.md`, the sim core in `src/context/`, the roadmap and the
GitHub milestones. Not read in depth: the UI pages, and the process ADRs (0012 to 0014, 0024) beyond their text.

## Verdict

- **A:** the code follows the ADRs. About a dozen ADR and doc statements are out of date, one change after ADR 0054
  breaks it, and ADR 0054 itself is only partly built.
- **B:** the core physics is realistic, but the playback speed is not the only workaround: four registered
  playability reductions remain, and a few recorded simplifications shape the start-up more than the register says.
- **C:** the steam-water cycle of a non-reheat drum unit is complete. The grid model, the auxiliary steam header and
  the casing metal temperature are missing, and none of the three has a ticket.

- Tests: `pnpm test` passes, 2087 tests in 120 files, no `test.fails`, no expected failures.

## B) Realism

### Sound

Pressure follows saturation; water mass is conserved to 1e-13; every stream enters at its real enthalpy; the flue gas
heat balance has three stages; the condenser pressure is vapour plus air with real-size vacuum pumps; the turbine speed
model has a sourced inertia and losses; the heating-rate limit is the real 100 K/h.

### Playability reductions besides the playback speed (in the register)

1. **Boiler volume halved:** 100 m³ against about 200 m³. The reason given is a full-gas cold start of 3.7 min
   instead of 8 to 9. Since #103 a correct start is paced by the 100 K/h limit (about 6 h) whatever the volume, so the
   reduction no longer buys playability; it only makes the drum react faster.
2. **UPS runtime:** 30 minutes against 8 to 10 hours.
3. **Gas knob:** 0 to full gas in 40 s; a real burner takes minutes.
4. **EMY diesel warm-up:** 60 s, marked playability.

The valve knob travel (15 s, real time) is a UI choice, not plant physics. Items 2 to 4 could move onto the playback
speed, which ADR 0034 brought in for this.

### Shortcuts with more effect than the register suggests

- **Gland steam from a cold drum.** Both cold starts seal the glands at t = 21 s from a 20 °C boiler. The draw is
  capped at `min(Flow · dt, steamMass)`, but the seal follows the switch, not the steam delivered
  (`PowerPlantTick.ts:86`, `:149`). The vacuum is pulled with sealed glands hours before there is steam to seal them.
  Recorded as "no draw limit by boiler pressure" (ADR 0047); a real plant uses the auxiliary steam header (ADR 0048 M2).
- **The roll onto a cold casing.** 540 °C steam into a 20 °C casing, a mismatch of about 520 K. Acknowledged by ADR 0048
  (M3).
- **A grid with a free speed.** With the generator breaker closed the speed still sags under load and the breaker opens
  at ±2 rpm; a grid holds the speed. Recorded in ADR 0027; ADR 0046 M1 replaces it.
- **Boiler metal 100 t, unsourced, and not in the register.** Own estimate, UNVERIFIED: the drum alone for 165 bar
  (about 1.7 m by 18 m) is about 100 t, and the waterwalls add several hundred tonnes, so the figure may be 3 to 5
  times low. It changes the pressure dynamics and the heat a start-up takes, not its time. Needs a research note.
- **No energy invariant.** Mass is checked to 1e-13; `sim/invariants.ts` has no energy balance, though ADR 0032 says
  the whole-plant balance can close. Known leaks: the gland steam heat (about 0.26 MW, untracked), #173 and pump work.
- **Speed PID naming (minor).** The PID is incremental (`position += Kp·e + Ki·∫e − Kd·dω/dt`), so its P term acts as
  integral action and the integral is clamped to ±1 rpm·s. It works (within 0.4 rpm of the reference), but the names
  mislead.

## Owner's take (27 November 2026)

- **A:** a documentation fix.
- **B 2 to 4** (UPS, gas knob, EMY warm-up): to be fixed.
- **B 1** (boiler volume): kept for now. The long-term plan is to replace the lumped boiler with a tube model, which
  changes the volume, the metal mass, the meaning of the level trips and the heating rate anyway.
- **C:** no new plant systems before the impact of the tube model is seen.
- **The grid** (Electrical M1, [ADR 0046](../adr/0046-from-island-to-town-grid-in-milestones.md)): the one exception to C, it
  goes ahead ([ADR 0057](../adr/0057-boiler-as-a-tube-model.md)). With the breaker closed the speed still sags under load
  and the breaker opens on a 2 rpm sag; a grid holds the speed, and a lack of steam only costs MW.

| [plant/turbine.md](../plant/turbine.md), phase 5 | The synchroscope plan (now M4) | plan only, old |

The M1 research note has to cover, before M1 is drafted:

1. **Reverse power:** the threshold (% of rating) and the delay, and the sequential trip (after a turbine trip the
   breaker waits for reverse power before it opens).
2. **The load controller:** a MW loop with a frequency bias, the load reference ramp (3 %/min today), valve position
   against power.
3. **Boiler-follow, turbine-follow or coordinated control,** and how fast the pressure may move: the main new operator
   task.
4. **Auto-sync windows:** slip, phase angle, voltage, and the time to close.
5. **The stiff grid:** the speed held at exactly 3000 rpm, or a large but finite inertia (the real frequency comes with
   M3).
6. **What stays out:** excitation and reactive power, stated as out for M1 or not.

Then a grilling, ADR 0027 rewritten in place (ADR 0046 says M1 does that), and the M1 tickets.
