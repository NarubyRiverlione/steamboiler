Heavy breaker with rocker handle

One rocker handle in a cast body, a big position flag, a nameplate and the existing flip cover in its hazard housing. Same props as TurnSwitch (on, onToggle, disabled, title, guard). Everything below is clickable: lift a cover, rock the handle, and change the demo conditions.

Notes and open points

  • Red closed and green open follows switchgear convention, but the rest of the board uses red for an alarm. A closed breaker is not an alarm, so the flag uses a darker red than --board-zone-alarm; the lamp and LCD colours stay untouched.
  • The cover works as in ADR 0039: down, the handle is blocked and a click or Space lifts it; up, the handle works and the strip (or Escape) lowers it. It is UI state only.
  • The UPS breaker is the one control that works on a dead vital bus, so its handle and cover must not depend on vital power (#143, #144). Here it is shown with no refusal.
  • No spring-charged flag, tripped flag or draw-out position: the sim has no such states.
  • The full-size body is about 250 px wide; the generation page holds three groups, so check narrow screens in the real board.css.
