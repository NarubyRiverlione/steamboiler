# The auxiliary bus is fed by the grid, the generator or the diesel

**Status: accepted.** Decided in #141, M2 of [ADR 0046](0046-from-island-to-town-grid-in-milestones.md); breaker refusals reworked after the research and grilling of #174 ([research note](../research/2026-11-breaker-interlocks-and-protections.md)); the generator's path split in two by #189 ([research note](../research/2026-11-unit-aux-breaker.md)). Builds on the auxiliary load of #140; amends nothing in [ADR 0027](0027-generator-load-is-a-resisting-torque.md), whose gross load and torque stay as they are.

The pumps need power. The **Auxiliary bus** is fed by a **Grid supply breaker** (`gridSupplyBreaker` in `PowerPlantState`), by the **Unit aux breaker** (`unitAuxBreaker` in `TurbineState`, #189), by the **Diesel breaker** (#169), or any together. The bus is **live** when any is closed; this is derived each tick (`isBusLive`), never stored. With all three open it is a **Dead bus**. The **Generator breaker** is the generator's tie to the grid (synchronising and the export go through it) and feeds no bus.

```
               GRID
       ┌────────┴─────────┐
 [Generator          [Grid supply
  breaker]            breaker]        station supply only, no export
       │                  │
 Generator ─┐             │
            │             │
   [Unit aux breaker]     │       [Diesel breaker]── Diesel
            │             │              │
   ═════════╧══ Aux bus ══╧══════════════╧══
```

## Decisions

- **The grid supply breaker starts closed and the unit aux breaker open** in every scenario and in a new game, until grid-loss scenarios come, so the golden runs do not change (the snapshots only gain the field). A dark-start scenario (`dark-start`) starts the grid supply open.
- **Closing is always allowed** (stiff grid, no sync check; the missing sync-check is a known gap in [real-plant-gaps.md](../plant/real-plant-gaps.md) until the grid model of ADR 0046 M1). Two reducer actions, `CLOSE_GRID_SUPPLY_BREAKER` and `OPEN_GRID_SUPPLY_BREAKER`.
- **The generator carries the bus** whenever the unit aux breaker is closed. Gross load stays the shaft load with the generator breaker closed: no new torque, the auxiliary load only gates the pumps and reads in net output, gross minus the auxiliary load whichever breaker feeds the bus (ADR 0027, #140).
- **The unit aux breaker** closes only with the turbine inside the ±2 rpm window and auto-opens when the speed leaves it, like the generator breaker (ADR 0027), until frequency and sync are modelled (later: on loss of sync); opening it by hand is never refused. Its switch is on the Auxiliary bus page.
- **Seamless transfer, not a plain interlock** (#189, owner's decision): the grid supply and unit aux breakers are never closed together for longer than a tick, but moving the bus between them never passes through a dead bus. Closing the unit aux breaker opens the grid supply breaker on the same tick; closing the grid supply breaker while the unit aux breaker is closed opens the unit aux breaker on the same tick (with the generator breaker open the unloaded generator goes back to Free, like any breaker opened by hand). Not fully realistic (a real plant uses a fast or make-before-break transfer); it stands in until frequency and sync are modelled. The diesel is not part of the transfer.
- **Opening the generator breaker is never refused.** A hand can always open a breaker; a real plant protects against the consequence afterwards, it does not refuse the action (#174). With the unit aux breaker closed the generator then carries the auxiliary bus alone, **house load operation** (the island: generator breaker open, unit aux breaker closed): the auxiliary load is a real load on the shaft and the unit carries what the bus draws, like an isochronous governor (the synchronised speed mode, Hold at 3000 rpm, stays in force). **The load steps to the auxiliary load on that tick, with no ramp** (#182 grilling): opening the generator breaker at a high load is a load rejection, the speed rises out of the ±2 rpm window, the unit aux breaker auto-opens and the bus is dead; only a low load survives the step. Closing the unit aux breaker with the generator breaker open enters house load operation at once. With too little steam the speed falls, leaves the ±2 rpm window, the unit aux breaker auto-opens and the bus is dead. No new underfrequency trip: no sourced numbers for one. Opening the unit aux breaker with nothing else feeding the bus is a dead bus at once.
- **No load setpoint floor.** The load setpoint needs the generator breaker closed, so in house load operation (generator breaker open) its control is disabled; opening the generator breaker sets it to 0, as ADR 0027 says. With both the generator and the unit aux breaker closed the generator exports gross minus the auxiliary load; a setpoint below the auxiliary load gives a negative net output (an import), as it does with the setpoint at 0 today. #139's 10 MW basic load was closed as not planned.
- **Control power (#143, #174, ADR 0055).** Breaker commands need the **safety bus** (fed from the Auxiliary bus, or by the battery on a dead one, ADR 0055, #190): with it dead, open and close do nothing (the coils are dead; no refusal message, the board shows the dead bus). Each breaker has a **local mechanical trip** that works with no bus and only opens (the unit aux breaker has none yet); there is no local close. It sits on the breaker in the switchgear room, so the control board has no control for it (#175 triage); a future switchgear page may add one. The **trip reset** needs the safety bus. The controller controls (load setpoint, speed mode, run-up point) need the **vital bus**: with it dead they freeze at their last state and ignore commands. The gland steam valve by hand and the turning gear motor (auxiliary bus, above) are as before. The ±2 rpm auto-open needs no bus: it models the loss of synchronism, not a relay trip. The bus assignments other than the breakers are owner decisions, not sourced.
- **A dead bus stops every pump:** the feed, condensation, recirculation and air extraction pumps and the lube oil pump deliver nothing, the turning gear motor stops and the gear drops out (like the low-oil dropout of [ADR 0051](0051-turning-gear-holds-the-stopped-rotor-and-blocks-the-valve.md); it stays out and is engaged again by hand), and the auxiliary load is 0. The stored valve positions, the oil pump switch, the air pump switch and the latched trip flags are untouched: `PowerPlantTick` hands the sub-ticks a plant with the pumps stopped and puts the operator's positions back afterwards. The turning gear cannot be engaged on a dead bus.
- **No special blackout trip.** The existing protections act (the island of a short unit ends in the speed-window auto-open above): low water (the burner trip), and the low lube oil trip of [ADR 0050](0050-lube-oil-pressure-permissive-and-trip.md) on a rolling turbine. There is no emergency DC oil pump until #159. Same stance as ADR 0027: failures need no special logic. The generator and unit aux breakers auto-open when the speed leaves the ±2 rpm window (ADR 0027), which is how a dead bus arises with the grid supply breaker open.
- **The onsite diesel generator (#169)** is a third supply, through a **Diesel breaker**, for a dark start. The bus is live when any of the three breakers is closed; a dead bus has all three open. Decided in the #169 grilling:
  - **No shaft yet:** the diesel holds exactly 50 Hz up to its rating; a shaft with inertia and a governor is #185. It has three states, stopped, starting and running: a start/stop switch, and on RUN it is starting for a fixed start time (sourced, or marked UNVERIFIED), then running. Starting needs no plant bus (its own starting battery, not modelled).
  - **Closing the diesel breaker** only while the diesel runs. Onto a dead bus: free. Onto a grid-fed bus: always allowed. Onto a bus fed by the unit aux breaker alone: only while the turbine is within the ±2 rpm window, otherwise refused with a reason (an interlock, [ADR 0054](0054-status-by-lamps-not-text.md)); the generator breaker's own rule is unchanged. It may close while the unit aux or the grid supply breaker is closed: the stand-in for auto-sync, so a diesel can be ready for a later grid-loss scenario (#189). Closed by hand; no auto-sync.
  - **The stiffer source carries the bus:** grid over generator (unit aux breaker) over diesel; a paralleled diesel runs unloaded. No droop sharing until the moving frequency of ADR 0046 M3.
  - **Overload is a consequence:** when the auxiliary load passes the rating while the diesel is the sole supply, the diesel breaker opens on that tick and the diesel keeps running. Not a latched trip, no reset; closing again with the load still too high opens it again. This replaces the speed fall of #174 until #185.
  - **STOP with the breaker closed** opens the breaker on that tick; no refusal.
  - **Rating:** the largest auxiliary load at start-up flows in the `dark-start` scenario, measured with the headless runner (1.99 MW), plus a stated margin (0.5 MW, 2.5 MW in all), both in [design-basis.md](../plant/design-basis.md). Fuel is #186. The breaker follows the safety bus rules of #184 like the others; until #184 is in, the diesel has its own local mechanical trip (`LOCAL_TRIP_DIESEL_BREAKER`: only opens, no bus, the same open path).
- **Resume without a reset:** when the bus is fed again the pumps run at their operator-set positions, and the lube oil pump runs if it is switched on. A lost supply is not a protective trip, unlike [ADR 0043](0043-trip-reset.md); latched trips still need their reset.

## Considered options

- **A net load setpoint** (the operator sets the net output, the generator adds the auxiliary load): rejected. It changes what the setpoint means, breaks the gross torque model of ADR 0027 and hides the auxiliary load the operator should see. The setpoint stays gross and net output stays a reading.
- **A blackout trip:** rejected, see above: the existing protections are enough and a new trip needs its own reset rules.
- **Resetting the pumps to zero on a dead bus:** rejected. A dead bus is not a trip; the operator's positions are kept and the plant resumes when the bus is fed.

## Consequences

- `PowerPlantState` gains `gridSupplyBreaker`; snapshots were regenerated for the field. `auxiliaryLoadOf` returns 0 on a dead bus.
- House load operation is built (#182, moved to the unit aux breaker by #189): `isHouseLoad` in `AuxiliaryBus.ts`, the auxiliary load passed to `TurbineTick`, which takes it as the shaft load with the generator breaker open and the unit aux breaker closed; `gridSupplyOpenRefusal`, `loadSetpointFloorRefusal` and `houseLoadRefusal` are gone. The unit aux breaker is `unitAuxBreaker` in `TurbineState` (snapshots regenerated for the field), actions `CLOSE_UNIT_AUX_BREAKER` and `OPEN_UNIT_AUX_BREAKER`. Edge scenarios `house-load-hold`, `load-rejection`, `house-load-short` and `unit-aux-transfer` (the pumps never stop).
- The onsite diesel is built (#169): `Diesel` and `dieselBreaker` in `PowerPlantState` (snapshots regenerated for the fields), `src/context/Diesel.ts`; `dark-start` recovers with the diesel, edge scenario `diesel-overload`. The page is built (#175): `src/components/Diesel/`, see `docs/dev/ui-board.md`.
- The grid supply and unit aux breaker switches are on the Auxiliary bus page (#142, #189); the app starts with the grid supply closed and the unit aux open.
- The full dark start up to the generator is #187. Transformers and excitation stay in [real-plant-gaps.md](../plant/real-plant-gaps.md).
