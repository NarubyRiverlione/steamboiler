# The auxiliary bus is fed by the grid or the generator

**Status: accepted.** Decided in #141, M2 of [ADR 0046](0046-from-island-to-town-grid-in-milestones.md); breaker refusals reworked after the research and grilling of #174 ([research note](../research/2026-11-breaker-interlocks-and-protections.md)). Builds on the auxiliary load of #140; amends nothing in [ADR 0027](0027-generator-load-is-a-resisting-torque.md), whose gross load and torque stay as they are.

The pumps need power. The **Auxiliary bus** is fed by a **Grid supply breaker** (new, `gridSupplyBreaker` in `PowerPlantState`), by the **Generator breaker**, or both. The bus is **live** when either is closed; this is derived each tick (`isBusLive`), never stored. With both open it is a **Dead bus**.

## Decisions

- **The grid supply breaker starts closed** in every scenario and in a new game, so the golden and edge runs do not change (the snapshots only gain the field). A dark-start scenario (`dark-start`) starts it open.
- **Closing is always allowed** (stiff grid, no sync check; the missing sync-check is a known gap in [real-plant-gaps.md](../plant/real-plant-gaps.md) until the grid model of ADR 0046 M1). Two reducer actions, `CLOSE_GRID_SUPPLY_BREAKER` and `OPEN_GRID_SUPPLY_BREAKER`.
- **The generator carries the bus** whenever its breaker is closed; a closed grid supply breaker is then the backup tie. Gross load stays the shaft load: no new torque, the auxiliary load only gates the pumps and reads in net output (ADR 0027, #140).
- **Opening the grid supply breaker is never refused.** A hand can always open a breaker; a real plant protects against the consequence afterwards, it does not refuse the action (#174). The grid supply breaker is the plant's tie to the grid (the export goes through it too), so the generator then carries the auxiliary bus alone, **house load operation**: the auxiliary load is a real load on the shaft and the unit carries what the bus draws, like an isochronous governor (the synchronised speed mode, Hold at 3000 rpm, unchanged). **The load steps to the auxiliary load on that tick, with no ramp** (#182 grilling): opening the tie at a high load is a load rejection, the speed rises out of the ±2 rpm window, the generator breaker auto-opens and the bus is dead; only a low load survives the step. Closing the generator breaker with the tie open enters house load operation at once. With too little steam the speed falls, leaves the ±2 rpm window, the generator breaker auto-opens (ADR 0027) and the bus is dead. No new underfrequency trip: no sourced numbers for one. Open with the generator breaker also open is a dead bus at once.
- **No load setpoint floor.** While the grid supply breaker is open the load setpoint is **ignored** (the unit carries the auxiliary load) and its control is disabled; the setpoint stays in state as the operator left it, and when the grid supply closes again the load ramps to it at the load rate (ADR 0027). #139's 10 MW basic load was closed as not planned.
- **Control power (#143, #174, ADR 0055).** Breaker commands need the **safety bus**: with it dead, open and close do nothing (the coils are dead; no refusal message, the board shows the dead bus). Each breaker has a **local mechanical trip** that works with no bus and only opens; there is no local close. The **trip reset** needs the safety bus. The controller controls (load setpoint, speed mode, run-up point) need the **vital bus**: with it dead they freeze at their last state and ignore commands. The gland steam valve by hand and the turning gear motor (auxiliary bus, above) are as before. The ±2 rpm auto-open needs no bus: it models the loss of synchronism, not a relay trip. The bus assignments other than the breakers are owner decisions, not sourced.
- **A dead bus stops every pump:** the feed, condensation, recirculation and air extraction pumps and the lube oil pump deliver nothing, the turning gear motor stops and the gear drops out (like the low-oil dropout of [ADR 0051](0051-turning-gear-holds-the-stopped-rotor-and-blocks-the-valve.md); it stays out and is engaged again by hand), and the auxiliary load is 0. The stored valve positions, the oil pump switch, the air pump switch and the latched trip flags are untouched: `PowerPlantTick` hands the sub-ticks a plant with the pumps stopped and puts the operator's positions back afterwards. The turning gear cannot be engaged on a dead bus.
- **No special blackout trip.** The existing protections act (the island of a short unit ends in the speed-window auto-open above): low water (the burner trip), and the low lube oil trip of [ADR 0050](0050-lube-oil-pressure-permissive-and-trip.md) on a rolling turbine. There is no emergency DC oil pump until #159. Same stance as ADR 0027: failures need no special logic. The generator breaker auto-opens when the speed leaves the ±2 rpm window (ADR 0027), which is how a dead bus arises with the grid supply breaker open.
- **The onsite diesel generator (#169)** is a third supply, through a **Diesel breaker**, for a dark start. The bus is live when any of the three breakers is closed; a dead bus has all three open. Decided in the #169 grilling:
  - **No shaft yet:** the diesel holds exactly 50 Hz up to its rating; a shaft with inertia and a governor is #185. It has three states, stopped, starting and running: a start/stop switch, and on RUN it is starting for a fixed start time (sourced, or marked UNVERIFIED), then running. Starting needs no plant bus (its own starting battery, not modelled).
  - **Closing the diesel breaker** only while the diesel runs. Onto a dead bus: free. Onto a grid-fed bus: always allowed. Onto a generator-fed bus: only while the turbine is within the ±2 rpm window, otherwise refused with a reason (an interlock, [ADR 0054](0054-status-by-lamps-not-text.md)); the generator breaker's own rule is unchanged. Closed by hand; no auto-sync.
  - **The stiffer source carries the bus:** grid over generator over diesel; a paralleled diesel runs unloaded. No droop sharing until the moving frequency of ADR 0046 M3.
  - **Overload is a consequence:** when the auxiliary load passes the rating, the diesel breaker opens on that tick and the diesel keeps running. Not a latched trip, no reset; closing again with the load still too high opens it again. This replaces the speed fall of #174 until #185.
  - **STOP with the breaker closed** opens the breaker on that tick; no refusal.
  - **Rating:** the largest auxiliary load at start-up flows in the `dark-start` scenario, measured with the headless runner, plus a stated margin, both in [design-basis.md](../plant/design-basis.md). Fuel is #186. The breaker follows the safety bus rules of #184 like the others.
- **Resume without a reset:** when the bus is fed again the pumps run at their operator-set positions, and the lube oil pump runs if it is switched on. A lost supply is not a protective trip, unlike [ADR 0043](0043-trip-reset.md); latched trips still need their reset.

## Considered options

- **A net load setpoint** (the operator sets the net output, the generator adds the auxiliary load): rejected. It changes what the setpoint means, breaks the gross torque model of ADR 0027 and hides the auxiliary load the operator should see. The setpoint stays gross and net output stays a reading.
- **A blackout trip:** rejected, see above: the existing protections are enough and a new trip needs its own reset rules.
- **Resetting the pumps to zero on a dead bus:** rejected. A dead bus is not a trip; the operator's positions are kept and the plant resumes when the bus is fed.

## Consequences

- `PowerPlantState` gains `gridSupplyBreaker`; snapshots were regenerated for the field. `auxiliaryLoadOf` returns 0 on a dead bus.
- House load operation is built (#182): `isHouseLoad` in `AuxiliaryBus.ts`, the auxiliary load passed to `TurbineTick` as the shaft load; `gridSupplyOpenRefusal` and `loadSetpointFloorRefusal` are gone. No new `PowerPlantState` field, snapshots unchanged. Edge scenarios `house-load-hold`, `load-rejection`, `house-load-short`.
- The grid supply breaker switch is on the Auxiliary bus page (#142); the app starts with it closed.
- The onsite diesel generator was split off to #169, which adds it to this ADR (above); `dark-start` becomes the diesel scenario, the full dark start up to the generator is #187. Transformers and excitation stay in [real-plant-gaps.md](../plant/real-plant-gaps.md).
