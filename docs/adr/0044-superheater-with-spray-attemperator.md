# A passive flue gas superheater, with one spray attemperator holding the steam at 540 °C

The plant sent saturated steam at 165 bar to the turbine, so the exhaust was about 35% wet at the design vacuum (a departure in [design-basis.md](../plant/design-basis.md); sourced limits are 10 to 15%). A superheater between the boiler and the main steam valve fixes most of that and adds the one control task of the steam side: the steam temperature. Decided in #113 ([research](../research/2026-11-superheater.md)).

## The model

- **Superheater:** a sized heat exchanger in the flue gas path, **before the boiler stage** (the third stage of [ADR 0029](0029-boiler-efficiency-from-flue-gas-heat-balance.md); the gas after the boiler stage is only about 100 K above the water, too cool to superheat). Gas enters it at the combustion temperature. No valve in the steam path: its heat follows from the gas, the surface and the steam flow, as in the economizer. Counterflow, log-mean difference, bisection on the steam outlet temperature. The surface scales with gas flow to the power 0.65. It is sized so 540 °C leaves it at full gas with the design steam flow (134 kg/s) and no spray: 101 kW/K, about 113 MW. The boiler stage is sized again for the colder gas it receives (561 kW/K, still 100 K above the water at full gas); the economizer is unchanged. The superheater heat is part of the heat into the water (the efficiency still means fuel against stack), but it leaves the gas before the boiler stage and goes into the steam outside the drum, not into the boiler energy.
- **Steam temperature:** one new lumped state, a first-order lag of 100 s (steam and tube metal together, no separate metal state: [ADR 0019](0019-boiler-is-one-lumped-vessel.md) holds). It lags toward what the superheater gives and the spray takes away, and never falls below the saturation temperature of the boiler pressure. All steam leaving the drum through the main steam valve (turbine valve and bypass) passes the superheater; the relief valve and the deaerator let-down valve take their steam at the drum and do not. With no flow the steam is saturated. The steam table ends 350 K above saturation: the steam never passes it (tube overheating at low flow is out of scope).
- **Attemperator:** a valve that injects feedwater into the steam between the superheater stages. A PID on the steam temperature, fixed 540 °C setpoint, derivative on the measurement ([ADR 0010](0010-pid-derivative-on-measurement.md)), integral clamped between 0 and its limit (the spray only cools). **Kd is 0**: the 100 s lag does not need it and a derivative chattered at the 0.1 s tick (a two-tick limit cycle of the valve, measured). Auto and Manual: in Manual the operator sets the valve and the integral follows, so switching back to Auto is bumpless; in Auto the operator cannot move it (like a latching control, [ADR 0039](0039-latching-controls-are-turn-switches.md), but a position, so two actions: `SET_ATTEMPERATOR_AUTO` and `SET_SPRAY_VALVE_POSITION`, a delta like the other valves). The spray is a valve flow: position times 15 kg/s.
- **Spray water:** from the feed pump discharge, before the extraction heater. It cannot exceed what the pump delivered in the last tick. It is taken out of the flow that reaches the boiler (the heater heats the rest) and added to the steam flows to the turbine and the bypass, so the mass balance ([ADR 0007](0007-water-state-is-mass-in-kg.md)) closes: the water in the loop is conserved with the spray running. The steam flows in the turbine state therefore count the spray; steam out of the boiler is those flows less `Boiler.sprayFlow`.
- **Turbine:** the isentropic drop uses the superheated steam (pressure and temperature) instead of the saturated steam ([ADR 0022](0022-electric-output-from-enthalpy-drop.md)); the tap enthalpy, the exhaust enthalpy and the exhaust quality follow ([ADR 0028](0028-exhaust-splits-by-quality-at-condenser-intake.md), [ADR 0031](0031-one-extraction-heater.md)). A **separate superheated steam table** (`steamSuperheatedData.ts`, IAPWS-IF97 region 2, 1 to 200 bar in rows 12% apart, columns by superheat above saturation, interpolated in ln(pressure) and superheat) gives enthalpy and entropy. The saturated tables are untouched ([ADR 0025](0025-finer-steam-table-for-density-slope.md)); with no superheat every function falls back to them.
- **Bypass and condenser:** bypass steam enters the condenser at the steam enthalpy, with no bypass desuperheating spray ([ADR 0032](0032-limited-condenser-and-stream-enthalpy.md) charges the real stream enthalpy).
- **Protection:** a high steam temperature alarm at 555 °C (15 K over the setpoint, warning). No trip.

## Considered options

- **A valve in the steam path of the superheater:** rejected. The real one is passive; only the outlet temperature is controlled.
- **A separate metal temperature state:** rejected. One lag of 100 s gives the response of the research note (100 to 200 s) without a second state.
- **The spray after the superheater (a desuperheater on the main steam line):** rejected for the model. Between the stages is where it is in a real plant, and the lumped superheater takes the mixed result.
- **Superheat in the saturated table (extending `steamEntropyData.ts`):** rejected. A separate table keeps ADR 0025 and every saturated figure.
- **Reheat in this ticket:** left for a later ticket. The exhaust is still about 17% wet.

## Consequences

- Built in #113: design steam flow and **rated steam flow 134 kg/s** (192 saturated), **rated output 152.4 MW** at the 31 mbar vacuum (141.9 MW saturated). Full gas is first reached at about 146 MW and 133 kg/s, and the most the plant carries on its stored heat is 166 MW (`max-output`). The plant efficiency at 125 MW is 34.9% against 31.4% with saturated steam and no heater. The exhaust at rated is **17% wet** (35% before; the hand estimate was 20 to 25%); reheat or a lower vacuum would be the next step, and 17% is still over the sourced 10 to 15%.
- The relief valve capacity is about 440 kg/s (457 before): with no steam flow the superheater takes nothing, and the gas enters the boiler stage hotter than its design inlet, so the worst-case heat into the water fell. [ADR 0005](0005-relief-valve-vents-to-atmosphere.md) and [ADR 0030](0030-return-pays-real-enthalpy.md) are unchanged in rule.
- The cold start and the heat-up are about 3 points less efficient at the boiler stage (89% instead of 93% at full gas with no Return and no steam): the superheater has no steam to heat before the main steam valve opens, and the gas arrives at the boiler stage hotter than it is sized for. `relief-lift` lifts at about 269 s instead of 259 s.
- The steam temperature is held to about 540 ± 1 K at load (the PID, `turbine-load`) and at 540.0 °C in `bypass-loop`. With the spray stuck shut at 125 MW (`spray-stuck-closed`) it rises to about 558 °C: the alarm fires, nothing trips, and the plant stays inside its limits.
- The spray is a new draw on the feed pump: about 2 kg/s at 125 MW, 4 kg/s at the bypass-loop hold. The pressure hold of the scenario operators sees a bigger dip when the load climbs starts (up to 4.7 bar at 149 s in `max-output`, 2.5 bar before): the superheater takes its heat from the first kg of steam before the gas catches up.
- Operator settings changed to fit the plant: the `bypass-loop` bypass is 6% (about 13 kg/s) instead of 10%, because the heat-up gas (2000 L/s) cannot also carry the 80 MW the superheater and its spray take at 20 kg/s; `return-stop` opens the bypass 50% instead of 70% (100 kg/s against the 134 kg/s of full gas, as 140 against 192 before).
- The turbine valve (200 kg/s), the relief valve and the condenser utilities are over-dimensioned for 134 kg/s; each is recorded in [design-basis.md](../plant/design-basis.md).
- Not built (out of scope, separate tickets): reheat, a bypass spray, an outlet safety valve, start-up vent and drains, a trip on high steam temperature, tube overheating at low flow, the UI (gauge, spray valve control, alarm tile).
