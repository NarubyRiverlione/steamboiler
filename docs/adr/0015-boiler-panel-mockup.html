Boiler Panel Mockups

Boiler panel: two ideas

Mockup for ADR 0010, which proposes a UI redo and decides nothing yet. Both panels show the same plant state: turn the gas on either one and the other follows. The plant numbers are illustrative only. A rough stand-in moves them; it is not the simulator. Limits come from the design basis: 165 bar working pressure, relief valve at 178 bar (closes at 171), heating-rate alarm at 0.4 K/s.
